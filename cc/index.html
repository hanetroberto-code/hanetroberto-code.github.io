How it started

It all began with something I'd heard too many times.

After a few disappointments in life, I found myself thinking about the things you hear people say all the time. At the bar, among friends, on social media. This one more than any other:

“Be careful: she's a woman. If you don't give her everything she wants, she can ruin you. And society doesn't care about your side of the story anymore: she can say whatever she wants.” — something you hear, over and over

So I thought: men need a tool to protect themselves. Something that shows clearly what really happened, and what two people actually said to each other. And I started building it.

As I built it, I realised I was wrong. I was thinking too small.

It isn't only men who need protecting. It's the children who disappear. The women killed at home by someone who said he loved them. The people who slip away in silence because no one noticed they were hurting. All of humanity needs a tool that helps it protect itself.

— Roberto

What it is

Two friends working together to keep you safe.

ConsensualCheck

The silent guardian

It lives in your phone and, only if you switch it on, it listens. If you're scared and say “help”, even in a whisper, even with the phone in your pocket, it understands.

It alerts the people you trust, tells them where you are and, if needed, calls emergency services. Meanwhile it keeps a record of what is happening, so no one can say it isn't true.

Trinitas

The guardian angel

It's the part that knows you. Over time it learns how you speak, how you usually are, what makes you feel good. So it notices when something is wrong, even if you don't say it.

It doesn't judge you, doesn't sell you anything, doesn't bury you in questions. It speaks only when it matters. And it gets there before it's too late.

Calls for help for you

One word is enough. The right people know straight away that you need them, and where to find you.

Notices the signs

If someone is shouting near you, or your voice sounds different from usual, it notices. Even when you can't say it.

Keeps the evidence safe

What it records is sealed with the date and time. After that, no one can change it. Not even you.

Clear agreements, signed by both

When two people agree on something important, a job, a rental, a relationship, they can say “yes” clearly, with their own fingerprint. And a yes can always be taken back.

Explains what you sign

Before you sign a contract, Trinitas explains it in simple words and tells you where to be careful.

Works without internet

Everything happens inside your phone. No faraway server listening, no one reading your secrets.

Our promises

Five things that will never change.

Everything stays on your phone.Your voice, your memories, your evidence are yours. No one else sees them.
Nothing without your yes.It only switches on if you want it to, and you switch it off whenever you like.
It protects, it doesn't watch.It's on your side. It doesn't monitor anyone else, and it isn't the police.
It replaces no one.Not emergency services, not a doctor, not a therapist. It helps you reach them sooner.
For everyone.The dream is for it to be free, like a seatbelt. A right, not a product.

Where we are

It already works. But one person isn't enough.

Today ConsensualCheck and Trinitas are a prototype that really works, on an Android phone. It listens, understands when you ask for help, recognises your voice and talks with you without internet. For now it speaks Italian.

You can't download it yet. Before it goes into the hands of people who are afraid, it has to be tested in the real world, checked by experts and made simple for anyone.

So far I've built it almost on my own. Now it needs a team.

Indie Open source No funding

ConsensualCheck is an independent, open-source project. We have no funding, no sponsors, and no money to pay anyone.

That's why we're looking for volunteers: people willing to give a little of their time and skills because they believe a tool like this should exist. The code will belong to everyone.
