The Hub

A world that breathes, and doors that pull you in

You start in the Hub: a floating psychedelic island where the ground ripples with plasma and every portal is a different game. No menus to fight — you walk up to a portal and you are in.

  • Third-person movement, jump, sprint and a color-drain blaster
  • Perception powers that bend the screen: monochrome trips, unsteady walks, shifting vision
  • Pick-ups that reward exploring instead of grinding
  • Invisible bounds that catch you if the void ever swallows you

Minigames

Arcade classics, put through the kaleidoscope

Every portal in the Hub leads somewhere else. Each game is rebuilt in 3D with its own rules, its own soundtrack and its own way of being ruined by a friend.

💠

PsyCrush

Match-3 that grows with your party: the board widens by six columns for every extra player. Match the same color as a friend within a heartbeat and the Resonance bonus doubles both scores.

Co-op · Competitive
🧱

PsyLine

Falling blocks on a 10×20 well, played over a floor of living psy-lines. Clean, fast, and mean once the speed climbs.

Arena · Score race
🫧

PsyPop

Shoot bubbles up an 8×8 grid, bank them off the side walls, watch the ceiling creep down. One wasted shot is one row closer to the end.

Arena · Score race
🏃

PsyRun

An endless three-lane runner through collapsing neon geometry. Real holes, real falls, no forgiving floor underneath.

PsyChomp

Roadmap

Worlds

Four places worth wandering off into

Not levels with an exit at the end — open zones you can drop into with friends and just mess around in.

Warzone Island

A jagged PvP island built for color combat: cover, sightlines and six spawn rings around the center. Home of the free-for-all mode.

Neon City

A vertical city drowning in signage and reflections. Rooftops, alleys and a skyline that never stops shifting hue.

Neon City II

The second district — wider streets, bigger drops, more room to run and shoot.

Psy Island

The calm one. Impossible flora, warm horizons, and the best place to find out what your perception powers really do to a landscape.

Online

Host a room, send a code, play

No lobby browser, no account to create. The host walks into the Hub and gets a room code on screen; anyone who types it in drops into the same world, the same portals, the same games.

  • Peer-to-peer connection — no dedicated server to rent or wait for
  • Everyone shares one persistent world session across every zone
  • Live cross-player scoreboards inside the puzzle arenas
  • Solo play is exactly the same game, just quieter

Marketplace

Earn PsyCoins. Own the drop.

Everything in the store is bought with coins you earn by playing. Music releases are minted in limited runs — each purchase writes a signed certificate carrying the copy number, the owner, the timestamp and the hash of the certificate before it.

🪙

PsyCoins

Earned from pick-ups and level-ups. No purchase path, no shortcut — the only way in is playing.

💿

Limited music

Five exclusive tracks across Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary tiers, from 5000 copies down to 100.

📜

Certificates

A SHA-256 chain records every copy sold, so an edition can be verified and never quietly reprinted.

🧑‍🎤

15 characters

Each with its own model, animation set and stats. Unlock them with levels or coins and switch any time.

🎚️

Full settings

Four audio buses, a built-in music player and ten rebindable actions, reachable from the menu and mid-game.
