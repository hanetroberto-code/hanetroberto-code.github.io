Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy covers the game Psychedelic World (Android and Windows) and this website. It is written to be read, not to be survived — if anything here is unclear, write to us and we will explain it in plain words.

The short version. You can play without giving us anything. The game creates an anonymous account by itself, with no email and no password. You only hand over an email address if you decide to, and we never sell anything to anyone.

Who is responsible

Psychedelic World is made by an independent developer based in Italy. For anything in this policy — questions, corrections, deletion — the contact address is hanetroberto@gmail.com.

What the game collects

DataWhenWhy
Account identifier Always, from the first launch The game creates an anonymous account so your coins, characters and progress survive a restart. It has no email and no password attached.
Email address Only if you choose to register So you can use the same progress on another device. Registering is optional; the game is fully playable without it.
Display name When you set one It is the name other players see in a room and on the leaderboards. Pick anything — it does not have to be your real name.
Game progress While you play Scores, level, experience and PsyCoins, so rankings and progression work.
Room codes and connection data Only in online play To put you and your friends into the same session. Kept only while the room exists.

The game does not ask for your contacts, your photos, your location or your microphone, and it does not read anything else on your device.

What this website collects

If you fill in the pre-registration form, we store the email address you type and the date. It is used for one thing: sending you the download link when the game is released. It is not passed to anyone and not used for anything else.

Advertising

The current release does not show advertising and contains no advertising SDK. If that changes, this page will be updated before it does, and this section will say exactly which network is used and what it collects.

Who processes the data for us

  • Supabase — accounts, progress and leaderboards. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored on their infrastructure.
  • Cloudflare — relay servers that let two players connect when their networks will not talk to each other directly. It passes game traffic through; it does not store it.
  • Google Play — distribution of the Android version. Google collects its own data under its own policy, which we do not control.

How long it is kept

Account and progress data is kept while the account exists. Pre-registration emails are kept until the launch mailing has been sent, and deleted afterwards. Connection data for an online room disappears with the room.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the data tied to your account, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted entirely — including the account itself. Write to the address above and say which account it is (the display name is enough). We will act on it within 30 days.

You can also stop everything at any time by uninstalling the game: that removes the local save from your device. The server-side account stays until you ask for it to be deleted.

How to delete your account → — the exact steps, what is removed, and what stays in backups and for how long.

Children

Psychedelic World is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters — new data, a new purpose, a new processor — the date at the top changes and the change is described here rather than slipped in quietly.
