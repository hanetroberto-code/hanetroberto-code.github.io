Delete your account

Psychedelic World · last updated 21 September 2026

This page explains how to have your Psychedelic World account, and everything stored against it, permanently deleted. It applies to the Android and Windows versions of the game.

How to ask

  1. Send an email to hanetroberto@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”.
  2. In the message, tell us the display name you use in the game. That is the name other players see in a room and on the leaderboards, and it is enough to find your account. If you registered with an email address, send the request from that address.
  3. We reply to confirm, and the deletion is carried out within 30 days of the request. You do not need an account, a login or a form to ask.

There is no charge, and you do not have to give a reason.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account identifier Deleted. This is the anonymous account the game creates on first launch.
Email address Deleted, if you had registered one. Nothing is kept for marketing.
Display name Deleted.
Game progress Deleted: PsyCoins, unlocked characters, ships, powers, level and experience.
Leaderboard entries Deleted, including your position in the world ranking.
Pre-registration email Deleted, if you had signed up on this website for the launch link.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept for our own use once an account is deleted. Two technical exceptions, both temporary:

  • Encrypted backups of the database may still contain the deleted records for up to 30 days after deletion, after which they are overwritten in the normal rotation. They are not read or used in that window.
  • Server logs may hold a technical record of requests for up to 30 days. These do not contain game progress.

After that, nothing remains.

Deleting only the local save

If you simply want the game gone from a device, uninstalling it removes the local save — coins, characters and settings stored on that device. This does not delete the server-side account, which stays until you ask for it using the steps above.
