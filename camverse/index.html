CamVerse

Shoot from every angle at once. Walk away with the edit already done.

Record from your Ray-Ban Meta glasses, your phone's cameras and a second phone simultaneously, then switch between them live while the camera rolls. What lands in your gallery is one continuous, already-cut video — no timeline, no post-production.

What it does

Built for one specific frustration: shooting the same moment from several angles, then losing an evening stitching it together.

🎬

Switch while recording

Tap any source thumbnail mid-take and the video cuts to it. Each switch becomes a timestamped segment, joined into one file.

🕶️

True POV

Your Ray-Ban Meta glasses become a camera source, so the hands-on shot is genuinely from your eyes rather than staged.

✨

Transitions built in

Fade, cross dissolve, slide left and right, zoom. Applied as you shoot, not bolted on afterwards.

🎨

Colour looks

Sepia, black & white, vivid, cool and warm, rendered straight into the recording.

📡

Go live

Stream to Twitch, YouTube and TikTok with your own stream key — several platforms at the same time if you want.

🔒

Stays on your phone

No account, no cloud, no upload. Recording happens locally and the files never leave the device unless you send them.

Cameras you can use

Up to four cameras in one recording — that is what has actually been tested, so that is what is claimed.
